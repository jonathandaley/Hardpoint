VESUVIUS

Heavy dreadnought HEAVY · DREADNOUGHT · primitive box model

The wall of the roster. Vesuvius is the widest, heaviest chassis — a broad slab torso on short, thick reverse-knee legs, carrying three weapon hardpoints. Its identity is internal heat: a furnace core burns in the chest and lava seams glow in every gap between armor plates — front and back, shoulders, hip line, brow. It carries three weapon hardpoints (twin shoulders plus a center chest mount); the weapons equip separately. Highest energy draw in the roster (e5.0). Scorched near-black armor over ember-red detail.
