The wrap, in order

130–60 primitives (boxes, cylinders) merged into one body mesh in Blender.
2Mesh is UV-unwrapped — every part becomes a flat island on one square atlas (128–256px).
3Each island is filled with its channel: armor on big panels, detail on struts/feet, a solid emissive island for the visor.
4Imported nearest-neighbor → the chunky pixels stay crisp on the model. Legs are a separate mesh/atlas so they can animate.

Rules

Pixel-art UV atlas, 4–5 colours per mech, nearest-neighbor (GV3)
One atlas = one draw call per body (GV4)
No triplanar panel-line shader — reads as wallpaper, ignores form (GV1)
No flat per-face colour as the final look — too little surface interest (GV2)
