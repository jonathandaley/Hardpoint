1 · ARMOR
Base albedo on body, waist, head, shoulders, legs. Carries the mech's identity colour. Unshaded — reads flat and graphic.
2 · DETAIL
Secondary tone for struts, vents, brackets, face panels. Usually a darker (or contrasting) sibling of armor.
3 · EMISSIVE (optional)
Black albedo + an emission colour at an energy multiplier. Visors, sensors, lava vents, shield emitters. This is the only channel that glows.
